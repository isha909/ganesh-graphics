Our Story

From a Hand-Crafted Sign Shop to a Full Manufacturer

Ganesh Graphics & Designers began in 2000 as a small, hand-crafted sign shop focused on high-quality indoor and outdoor signage. Over two decades, we've grown into a full manufacturing and printing operation — without ever losing the hands-on craftsmanship that built our reputation.

Today, we design, manufacture and install everything in-house, combining that original craft with modern technology to serve businesses across Gurugram and the NCR.
